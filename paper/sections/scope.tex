\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The headline experiments use binary classification, Morgan
fingerprints, and a $256$-unit MLP from scratch; the test-set
construction is fixed at a single canonical seed and $\lambda$ is
selected on BACE alone.  We do not vary the test-set seed, the
development dataset, the twin head count, or the multi-class output
type.  The numerical value of $\lambda$ does not transfer across
architectures unmodified, though the selection rule does
(Section~\ref{sec:generalisation}); a practitioner moving to a new
architecture re-runs a single sweep on a development dataset.
Bagging has no $\lambda$ and works on every architecture and
backbone we tested.
