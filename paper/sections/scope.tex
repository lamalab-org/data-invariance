\section{Scope and limitations}
\label{sec:scope}

\paragraph{Pretrained backbones.}
A pretrained backbone is itself an implicit data prior: large-scale
pretraining has collapsed most cross-sample variation in the learned
features.  Twin-indep adds the same prior on top of finetuning, and
the two pull the model toward an over-averaged solution.  Empirically,
on Waterbirds (ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50, $\lambda{=}300$ as
applied to chemistry) accuracy collapses by $27$pp while churn does
fall.  Bagging adds no second prior and is unaffected
($+2$pp accuracy, $-51\%$ relative churn).  An $N$-aware scaling
$\lambda_N = \lambda_{\mathrm{dev}} \cdot N_{\mathrm{dev}}/N \approx
60$ recovers some accuracy but does not reach the bagging Pareto
frontier; twin-indep should not be applied unmodified to
pretrained-backbone settings.

\paragraph{Inter-network consistency collapse on MOF-thermal.}
Strong consistency between two networks has a degenerate solution:
both predict the majority class everywhere, so they agree trivially.
On MOF-thermal, twin-indep and codistillation reach this solution
(id-acc $0.53$, chance on a binary task; ERM $0.62$); bagging and
ERM, neither of which couples the two networks, are unaffected
(Appendix~\ref{app:borderline}).  Twin-indep should not be applied
to small-$N$ datasets whose ERM accuracy is close to the
majority-class baseline; the headline filter we use ($\text{ERM-acc}
\geq \text{majority-acc} + 5\text{pp}$) is necessary but not
sufficient on its own to predict this collapse.

\paragraph{Untested settings.}
Our results are for binary classification with cross-entropy training,
Morgan fingerprints, and a $256$-unit MLP trained from scratch.  The
fragility definition reduces unchanged to multi-class argmax
disagreement and extends to regression with a metric substitution;
we have not verified the method or magnitudes there.  Twin-indep
with $K{=}2$ extends mechanically to larger $K$ via all-pairs sym-KL,
interpolating between bagging-$K$ and our $K{=}2$ twin; we do not
characterise this trade-off.  Whether the pretrained-backbone failure
mode reproduces under fine-tuned
ChemBERTa~\citep{TODO_chithrananda2020_chemberta} is a controlled
test summarised in Appendix~\ref{app:chemberta_scope};
D-MPNN~\citep{TODO_yang2019_dmpnn} is the obvious follow-up
architecture.

\paragraph{Protocol choices we did not vary.}
The reported numbers fix test-set construction at a single canonical
seed (\texttt{99}) and select $\lambda$ on a single development
dataset (BACE).  We did not re-run the sweep at additional canonical
seeds, so we cannot rule out test-set-construction sensitivity in
absolute fragility magnitudes; nor did we cross-validate the
development rule across multiple dev datasets.  Both robustness
checks are natural follow-ups.
