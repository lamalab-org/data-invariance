\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The main magnitudes and method comparison are on chemistry binary
classification (MoleculeNet, TDC ADME and Tox, materials-science).
Regression is covered for three MoleculeNet benchmarks
(Appendix~\ref{app:regression}); Waterbirds is a single vision
cross-check; multi-class classification and structured outputs are
not tested.  We use $K{=}2$ throughout twin-bootstrap and do not study larger head
counts.  

We measure cross-sample churn at the prediction layer.  Whether
reducing it translates to better end-to-end outcomes in
Bayesian-optimisation campaigns or active-learning loops --- where
more stable per-prediction probabilities should yield more
reproducible acquisition decisions across data refreshes --- is a
plausible but untested consequence of our results, and an obvious
follow-up.
