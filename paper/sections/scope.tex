\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The headline magnitudes and method comparison are on chemistry binary
classification (MoleculeNet, TDC ADME and Tox, materials-science).
Regression is covered for three MoleculeNet benchmarks
(Appendix~\ref{app:regression}); Waterbirds is a single vision
cross-check; multi-class classification and structured outputs are
not tested.  Pretrained-backbone results (ChemBERTa, ResNet-50, GIN)
are single-canonical-seed cross-checks, not full sweeps replicated
across canonical seeds.  We use $K{=}2$ throughout twin-bootstrap and
do not study larger head counts.
