\begin{abstract}
Two classifiers trained on independent bootstraps of the same
chemistry training set assign different classes to
$\churnMin\text{--}\churnMax\%$ of test molecules, while their
aggregate accuracy differs by only $\accDiffMin\text{--}\accDiffMax$
percentage points.  We call this \emph{cross-sample prediction
fragility}.  Parameter-side methods --- deep ensembles, Monte Carlo
dropout --- move the class-flip rate by $\paramSideLow\%$ to
$\paramSideHigh\%$ relative to empirical risk minimisation (ERM)
across \nDatasetsHeadline{} chemistry benchmarks, with no consistent
direction.  Methods that vary the data work: bagging cuts the rate
$\bagFiveLow\text{--}\bagFiveHigh\%$ on every dataset with no
accuracy regression.  \emph{Twin-bootstrap} --- two networks trained
on independent bootstraps with a soft consistency loss --- cuts a
further median $\twinMedianReduction\%$ at $2\times$-ERM compute and
reduces softmax-distribution disagreement by
$\sim\symKLTwinAdditionalFoldMedian\times$.  Among three operating
points on the codistillation family ($\overlapPctLo\%$,
$\sim\overlapPctMid\%$, $\overlapPctHi\%$ inter-network bootstrap
overlap), only $\sim\overlapPctMid\%$ reduces churn on every dataset;
the consistency-weight selection rule transfers unchanged across
MLP, GIN, ChemBERTa, and ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50.  For triage,
a single extra retraining ranks predictions: reviewing the top
$30\%$ catches $\triageKtwoLow\text{--}\triageKtwoHigh\%$ of all
retraining-induced flips.  In small-$N$ from-scratch scientific ML,
the gap to parameter-side methods is large enough to demand a
fragility column in the benchmark report.
\end{abstract}
