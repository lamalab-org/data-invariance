\begin{abstract}
Two classifiers trained on independent samples of the same chemistry
training population assign different classes to $8\text{--}17\%$ of
test molecules with no measurable change in aggregate accuracy.  We
call this \emph{cross-sample prediction fragility}: the population-level
analogue of \emph{prediction churn}~\citep{TODO_milani2016}.  Where
deployment-loop work measures churn between a new model and an
incumbent on web-scale data, we measure it between two equally
weighted bootstraps of the training pool, in the small-$N$ from-scratch
regime that scientific machine learning inhabits and where no incumbent
exists.  On nine chemistry benchmarks (MoleculeNet, TDC ADME/Tox,
materials science) fragility exceeds the parameter-variance axis
captured by deep ensembles and MC dropout by an order of magnitude.  $K$-bootstrap
bagging cuts argmax churn $40\text{--}54\%$ on every dataset with no
accuracy regression.  A sym-KL consistency loss between two networks
trained on independent bootstraps (\emph{twin-indep}) cuts argmax
churn by a further median $60\%$ (range $16\text{--}92\%$ across the
eight datasets) at $2\times$-ERM compute matched against
bagging-$K{=}2$, and reduces distributional disagreement by another
factor of ${\sim}8$.  Twin-indep is the codistillation family of
\citet{TODO_anil2018_codistillation} at $\sim$$40\%$ inter-network
bootstrap overlap; on a 10-dataset comparison across three operating
points on that family ($0\%$, $\sim$$40\%$, $\sim$$63\%$
inter-network overlap), only $\sim$$40\%$ holds accuracy on every
dataset.  The recipe applies in the small-$N$ from-scratch regime;
on pretrained backbones (Waterbirds with ImageNet ResNet-50;
ChemBERTa-77M-MTR fine-tuned on six chemistry benchmarks) the
consistency loss over-regularises features the backbone already
encodes.  Bagging and codistillation already exist; what this paper
adds is a measurement protocol that makes their cross-sample effect
visible, and a regime where the gap they close is large enough to
demand its own column in the report.
\end{abstract}
